# Brag Plan: Rastogi Traders (रस्तोगी ट्रेडर्स)

## What is this app?
Rastogi Traders is a trust-first, Hindi-first rural agricultural business web platform that connects North Indian farmers and dairy owners to authentic fertilizers, pesticides, seeds, and cattle feed with 1-tap WhatsApp quotes and crop agronomy advisory.

## The angle
Rural India's agricultural backbone meets slick digital commerce: No confusing cart checkouts, no slow forms — just authentic Devanagari copy, live stock availability, and instantaneous 1-tap WhatsApp quotes directly to the trusted local shopkeeper.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"स्थानीय दुकान + आधुनिक सुविधा" — A bold, high-contrast forest green banner slams in with a badge: "कृषि और पशु आहार का भरोसेमंद साथी", transitioning immediately into the live product search and 1-tap WhatsApp inquiry.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Moment 1: Authentic Hindi Agricultural Catalog** — Real agricultural products (IFFCO DAP, Coragen, Cattle Feed) with live stock badges (*दुकान पर उपलब्ध*) and clear category pills (*उर्वरक, कीटनाशक, पशु आहार, बीज*).
- **Moment 2: 1-Tap WhatsApp Customer Connect** — Simulated click on the WhatsApp button instantly generating a pre-filled Devanagari inquiry message: `"नमस्ते, मुझे Rastogi Traders से IFFCO DAP की कीमत और उपलब्धता जाननी है।"`.
- **Moment 3: Kisan Corner (किसान कॉर्नर)** — Crop agronomy advisory with stage-wise fertilizer schedule for Wheat (गेहूँ) and Paddy (धान).

## Outro / punchline
"दुकान तक पहुँचें या 1-क्लिक में आज का भाव पूछें।" — Crisp lockup of Rastogi Traders logo, phone number, and location map marker.

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry**: Farmer searches for `DAP खाद` or selects `उर्वरक` category.
2. **Key action**: Taps `📲 WhatsApp पर भाव पूछें` on the verified IFFCO product card.
3. **Result**: Pre-filled WhatsApp chat opens with exact product SKU and price query ready to send.
